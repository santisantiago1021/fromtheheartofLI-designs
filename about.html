¿Por qué ahora? · Why now

The Heart of Long Island Is Under Attack.

"Only the people save the people."

This International Workers' Day — a day rooted as much in global struggle as in solidarity — the Village of Hempstead called on communities from Long Island to New York City and beyond to join the ICE Out of Long Island: March for Humanity mobilization.

May Day is about workers, immigrants, organizers, and everyday people coming together to demand dignity, safety, and fair treatment. That history is not separate from this moment — it is a continuation. The same forces that exploit labor and divide communities are fueling fear and enforcement in places like Hempstead.

Stand with us — in defense of democracy, for an end to ceaseless detentions and deportations, for the dignity of our workers, unions, and immigrants — the very backbone of our society. International Workers' Day reminds us that this fight belongs to all of us.

¿Por qué Hempstead? · Why Hempstead

The Heart of Long Island.

The Village of Hempstead is home to nearly 60,000 people within Nassau County, Long Island, New York. It is one of the most segregated regions in the country — and one of the most intensely targeted by ICE.

Families are being separated. Community members are afraid to go to work, to school, and to move freely through their own neighborhoods. When one community is treated as disposable, it puts us all at risk.

When people come together across race, background, and struggle, we become a problem that cannot be ignored.

Nuestras demandas · Our demands

Put
Humanity
First.

01

Put Humanity First.

Stop mass deportations, detention, and forced disappearances. The dignity of every person — regardless of where they were born or how they arrived — is non-negotiable.

02

Our Dignity Is Not Negotiable.

Protect the dignity of workers, unions, and immigrants. We are the backbone of society. Our contributions, our lives, and our presence are not up for debate.

03

No Child Detention. Ever.

There is no humane way to detain a child. Period. Not one that feels better. Not one with better conditions. None.

04

Not One Detention Center.

Not in our Village. Not on Long Island. We oppose the expansion of the carceral infrastructure that makes mass detention possible.

05

ICE Out of Long Island.

End all collaboration with ICE: here in Hempstead, across Nassau County, and throughout Long Island. No cooperation. No cover. No complicity.

Lo que hemos visto · Documented examples of harm

What Is Happening in Hempstead.

Cases below have been corroborated by our trusted local ICE Watch committee, cross-referenced through impacted family outreach and ICE detention status updates. Public testimony, images, and video available upon request.

Illegal Abductions & Racial Profiling

Legally-documented residents and neighbors with no criminal records have been detained and deported. ICE has staked out bus stops and terminals to racially profile and detain people of Hispanic appearance.

Fabricated Warrants

Documentation of warrants being fabricated after abductions to justify illegal detentions. Video footage available of cases where ICE detained people regardless of status and readily available documentation.

Village & Police Cooperation

Documented use of the local police armory for temporary detentions. Documented police collaboration with ICE. Unnecessary traffic tickets issued to legal observers monitoring ICE activity.

Targeting Community Spaces

ICE crackdowns at the Home Depot parking lot — a center for the day labor community. Arrests at Latino-owned delis, grocers, restaurants, and businesses. Documented arrests as early as 6:30am on Christmas Eve morning.

Violence at Point of Arrest

A 32-year-old Honduran resident's head was smashed into a brick wall upon arrest at a grocery store frequented by the Latino community. (January 5, Newsday)

Detentions Regardless of Status

Known cases of people with SIJS (Special Immigrant Juvenile Status) detained at the armory. Numerous documented cases of ICE arriving in unmarked vehicles and detaining people simply walking down the street.

Nuestra visión · Our goal

Our Collective Power.

"Our collective power shapes the just and equitable world we deserve. Not someday — but now."

We are sending a message: The Village of Hempstead and Long Island residents broadly will not stand for the forced disappearance, detention, or deportation of our families, friends, colleagues, and neighbors. We will not stand for the elimination of our community. We will not tolerate fear in our streets, or accept silence in the face of harm.

In Hempstead, community means all of us — from Indigenous, Black, and Latino peoples to immigrants and working class, to every neighbor and ally around and in between. Hempstead is home. Hempstead is all of us. We are the pulse — the heart — of Long Island.
