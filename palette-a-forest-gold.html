"Only the people save the people."

From the Heart of Long Island is a community space rooted in Hempstead, built on mutual aid, sustained organizing, and the belief that collective care is how neighborhoods thrive.

This is a space for connection — to resources, relationships, and work that outlasts any single campaign. When we hold each other up through whatever comes, we [PLACEHOLDER TEXT]...
